PALM//PLAY V1.6 — NEON ARCADE

Open index.html in Chrome for keyboard play, or deploy to an HTTPS site for webcam hand tracking.

Keyboard: Left/Right arrows move, 1=open, 2=fist, hold 3=freeze.
Camera: click Turn camera ON; train OPEN and FIST; PEACE can be detected from landmarks.
Target: score 100 in 60 seconds; 3 lives; combos and gold stars.

No API key is used. Hand tracking loads MediaPipe from CDN.
